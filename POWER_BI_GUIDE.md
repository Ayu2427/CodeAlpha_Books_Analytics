# Power BI Dashboard Guide

Load `data/books_clean.csv` into Power BI Desktop.

## KPI Cards
- Total Books: Count of Title
- Average Price: Average of Price_GBP
- Median Price: Median of Price_GBP
- Average Rating: Average of Rating_Num

## Visuals
1. Clustered column chart — Rating distribution
2. Column chart/histogram — Price distribution using price bins
3. Scatter chart — Rating_Num vs Price_GBP
4. Bar chart — Top 10 books by Price_GBP
5. Table — Title, Price_GBP, Rating, Availability
6. Slicers — Rating and Availability

## Dashboard title
**BOOKS TO SCRAPE — PRICE & RATING ANALYTICS**

Add a short insights text box and save as `Books_Analytics_Dashboard.pbix`.
